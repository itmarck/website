Minima

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

This policy describes how the applications developed by itmarck (Minima and Wingman) handle your information. We are committed to respecting your privacy and being transparent about how our apps work.

Minima

Minima is a minimalist launcher for Android. It operates with an offline-first architecture: your data is stored locally on your device and only synced with external services when you choose to configure it.

Data Minima stores

  • Drafts and tasks — saved in a local SQLite database on your device. They are only synced with Notion if you voluntarily provide an integration token.
  • App preferences — the configuration of favorite apps, hidden apps, and their order is stored exclusively on-device. It is never sent to any server.
  • Notion token — if you choose to connect Notion, the token is stored encrypted on your device using the operating system's secure storage. It is never shared with third parties.
  • Installed app list — Minima reads the apps installed on your device to display them. This list is not transmitted or stored outside your device.

Notion sync

Syncing with Notion is optional and requires you to manually configure an integration token. When active:

  • Drafts created locally are sent to your Notion database.
  • Tasks and subtasks are fetched from your Notion and cached locally.
  • Progress updates are synced back to Notion.

Communication happens directly between your device and the Notion API. There is no intermediary server operated by us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect personal or identifying data.
  • We do not use analytics, trackers, or third-party SDKs.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not share data with third parties (except the direct Notion sync you configure).
  • We do not store data on our own servers.

Wingman

Wingman is a companion application developed by itmarck. Like Minima, Wingman is designed with privacy as a priority.

Wingman does not collect personal data, does not use analytics or trackers, and does not share information with third parties. All information handled by the app stays on your device.

Permissions

Our applications only request the permissions strictly necessary for their operation. In the case of Minima as a launcher, this includes access to the list of installed apps and the ability to launch them.

Storage and security

All sensitive data (such as the Notion token) is stored using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. Local data (drafts, tasks, preferences) resides in a SQLite database within the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps.

Your rights

You can delete all local data at any time from the settings screen in Minima. When you uninstall any of our apps, all locally stored data is automatically removed.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the revised version on this page with a new update date. No changes that reduce your privacy rights will be made without prior notice.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at minima@itmarck.com.
