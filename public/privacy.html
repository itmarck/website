Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy applies to everything published by itmarck (Marcelo Velásquez): this website and the applications listed below. The general rules come first; each product then adds only what is specific to it.

General rules

  • We do not collect personal or identifying data.
  • We do not use analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, except services you choose to connect yourself.
  • Your data stays on your device unless a product says otherwise below.
  • Apps request only the permissions they need to work.
  • Sensitive data, such as access tokens, is kept in the secure storage provided by the operating system.

Your rights

You can delete local data at any time from each app's settings, or by uninstalling it, which removes everything stored on your device.

This website

  • No cookies, accounts or forms. Contact happens by email, from your own mail app.
  • Language choice — if you switch language, it is remembered in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Fonts — the typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Hosting — the site is served by Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs.

Minima

Minima is a minimalist launcher for Android. It works offline first: your data is stored on your device and only synced with external services you configure.

  • Drafts and tasks — saved in a local database on your device. Synced with Notion only if you provide an integration token.
  • App preferences — favorite, hidden and ordered apps are stored only on your device.
  • Notion token — if you connect Notion, the token is stored encrypted in the system's secure storage.
  • Installed apps — Minima reads the list of installed apps to show and launch them. The list never leaves your device.

Notion sync is optional. When active, drafts are sent to your Notion database, tasks are fetched and cached locally, and progress is synced back. Your device talks directly to the Notion API; there is no server of ours in between.

Wingman

Wingman is a companion application to Minima. It follows the general rules above and adds no data collection of its own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Changes that reduce your privacy are never made without notice.
