# Product

Personal site of Marcelo Velásquez (itmarck), software developer in Peru: frontend, backend and self-hosted infrastructure.

- **Goal:** show projects and professional experience, and make contact easy (contact@itmarck.com).
- **Audience:** recruiters, clients and other developers. English first, Spanish for Spanish-speaking browsers.
- **Main projects:** Minima (launcher for Android and desktop) and Wingman (personal log with its own API). Also Flutter Tree, Bambulapse, and work for Disney through Globant.
- **Constraints:** static site (Astro, Cloudflare). Fast and smooth on phones; motion is a feature but never at the cost of fluidity. No personal photo for now.
- **May grow into:** notes (blog), a lab for shader and 3D experiments, project case studies.
